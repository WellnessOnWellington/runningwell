Help Guide
Instructions and tips for using Running Well.
Quick reference for managing Running Well. All features below are desktop admin unless noted.
🪪Kiosk
  • Runs on the dedicated reception device — staff tap their RFID card to clock in or out
  • If someone clocks outside their rostered window, they're prompted to leave a note
  • Footer buttons give staff access to Who's On and Requests without manager involvement
👥Who's On
  • Read-only roster view for staff, accessed from the kiosk and protected by a PIN (set in Settings → Roster Settings)
  • Day view — Gantt timeline across all roles; darker bars indicate Clinic or Admin lane
  • Fortnight view — 2-week calendar grid; click a day to open the full day view
  • Person view — select a staff member to see their individual fortnight schedule
📋Staff Portal
  • Staff scan their card at the Requests screen (kiosk footer) to submit and track requests
  • Leave — goes directly to the manager for approval
  • Shift Swap — sent to a colleague first; if they accept, it comes to you for final decision
  • Give Away — transfers a shift to a colleague permanently; requires your approval
  • Approved swaps and give-aways automatically update the roster
🗂️Team
  • Add, edit, or deactivate staff from the Team tab
  • Each person needs a role (Receptionist, Nurse, or Manager) and an RFID card number
  • Use the role filter tabs to narrow the view
  • Deactivated staff are hidden from active views but their records are preserved
📅Roster
  • Build the two-week roster in fortnight blocks — invisible to staff until published
  • Drag a shift bar to move it; drag the edge to resize
  • Nurses can be assigned Floor or Clinic lane; Receptionists can be Floor or Admin
  • Use Week A / Week B tabs for alternating schedules
  • Hit Publish when the fortnight is ready — staff will see it in Who's On
📝Notes
  • Notes appear when staff clock in or out outside their rostered window (Arrival/Departure), clock in with no roster shift at all (Unrostered arrival), or report a break shorter than expected (Break note)
  • Each note shows the staff member, the time, and their reason
  • Arrival, Departure, and Break notes need a pay decision first — e.g. Unpaid, Annual Leave, Personal Leave, pay to roster time, or pay standard break — before Acknowledge is enabled
  • Flag for follow-up is always available, even before a decision is made, if you need to escalate rather than resolve it on the spot
  • Acknowledging an Arrival/Departure note locks the entry using the times or leave type you chose; acknowledging a Break note only settles the break question — the row may still need locking separately
🕐Timesheets
  • Review clocked hours against rostered hours per employee, per day, at the end of each fortnight
  • Flag colours: green = auto-approved or locked, yellow = needs review, red = missing clock-in or clock-out, orange = flagged for follow-up
  • Shifts close enough to rostered time auto-approve; anything else needs the row's own Lock button
  • Lock is blocked until any arrival/departure gap or short-break shortfall has a decision — resolve it right there on the row, or from the Notes tab
  • Use the payroll type dropdown to mark a day as leave or public holiday instead of worked time
  • Export to MYOB once entries are locked or auto-approved — each employee's header shows "Ready for MYOB" when every day is done
📊Analytics
  • Full audit logs for clock events, notes, requests, and timesheet adjustments
  • Filter each audit by staff member, role, and date range
⚙️Settings
  • Grace period — minutes either side of shift start/end before staff are prompted for a reason and the shift needs a manager decision instead of auto-approving
  • Red threshold — how many minutes further past the grace period escalates a timesheet row to a red flag
  • Coverage thresholds — staffing levels that show red/yellow/green on the roster
  • Who's On PIN — the PIN staff enter to access the Who's On roster view
  • Fortnight cycle start date — only change this during initial setup

Release History
v3.0
16 September 2026 — Leave That Cannot Quietly Disappear
Approving leave for dates before that roster had been built used to write nothing at all, and say nothing about it. The request was marked approved, the staff member was told it was approved, and no record existed anywhere that leave was ever due — so the roster could be built weeks later, over the top of it, by someone with no way of knowing. That is now closed from both ends. Every leave decision is recorded permanently in a new Leave Log, which says who decided it, when, and what it actually did — including when the answer is “nothing yet, because there is no roster”. It can be searched by name, filtered by leave type or by the dates of the leave, and it cannot be edited or deleted once written. The roster now shows leave it has not caught up with: a day cell flags anyone with approved leave and no shift, and flags anyone rostered to work on their own approved leave. Copying a fortnight forward lists any shifts that land on leave instead of copying them over it silently, adding a shift to a day with approved leave offers to mark it as leave, and publishing a roster lists anything still unresolved — the last cheap moment to notice, before staff have planned around it. Approved leave that has not happened yet is now listed in the Requests tab with how many days of it are applied, unrostered, or clashing with a shift, and it can be withdrawn, with the staff member notified and the reason recorded. Leave can no longer be changed in a pay period already sent to payroll, or removed once locked for payroll, or written onto a block that already carries worked time. Undoing a deleted leave record now genuinely restores it, which it did not before. In the Staff Portal, a leave request now tells you its outcome — approved, declined or withdrawn, by whom and when — instead of only the date you submitted it.
v2.8
7 September 2026 — Split Shifts Paid in Full
A day rostered as two separate blocks was only ever exported to payroll once, so the second block went unpaid. Both blocks are now paid, each gets its own timesheet row and its own line on the printed timesheet, and each is judged against the block it actually belongs to rather than the first one of the day. Two affected days were found in the existing records and corrected. Separately, “hours worked” was being worked out in several different places by several different methods, and only some of them matched what payroll pays — in one fortnight a staff member’s header total read 23.8 hours higher than the rows beneath it. There is now one definition of paid hours — snapped times, less the unpaid break, plus any paid leave — and the timesheet row, the fortnight total and the payroll export all read from it, so they cannot drift apart again. Clocking in early is no longer unlimited: beyond an hour early the kiosk asks why, exactly as it already did for lateness, and the entry goes to a manager instead of approving itself. Three hours early used to look identical to three minutes early, which is how a seven-hour day came to be paid as three and a half. The figure the kiosk shows at clock-out is now labelled as your two clock times and time on site, which is what it is — it is not your paid hours, because it deducts no break. Finally, the kiosk and Who’s On screens no longer re-read the entire database every minute, which was slowing older tablets.
v2.7
26 August 2026 — Swap Requests Stay Open Long Enough To Be Useful
A swap or give-away used to lapse seven days after it was sent, which got it wrong both ways: one raised three weeks ahead died a fortnight before the shift it was about, while one raised the day before a shift lapsed a week after the shift had already been worked. A request now stays open until the shift’s pay period has been reconciled — the Wednesday after the period ends — so a manager can still approve it and let the roster correct itself instead of editing the roster by hand. If a colleague never answers, the request no longer dies: it goes to the manager, marked as unanswered, because they are the one who can still put it right. Requests that did lapse now stay visible in your History instead of disappearing, and the Staff Portal no longer offers Accept on a request whose deadline has passed. Once a pay period has been finalised and sent to payroll, requests affecting it can no longer be approved — un-push the period first if it genuinely needs changing. Requests waiting on a manager now show how long they have been waiting, oldest first.
v2.6
26 August 2026 — Clearer Unpaid Time on Timesheets
Unpaid time no longer appears as its own line with a figure in the Hours column, where it read like hours owed when the meaning was the opposite — time not worked and not paid. It now reads as a note against the day itself, for example “Arrived 7 min after rostered start — unpaid”. Paid leave taken as part of a worked day is unchanged: annual, personal, compassionate and long service leave still appear on their own line with their hours, because those hours are genuinely paid from a leave balance and belong in your totals.
v2.5
24 August 2026 — Timesheet PDFs Match Payroll
Downloaded timesheet PDFs now state exactly what payroll pays. A leave day was printing the full rostered span, and an early arrival was printing the clock time — both showed more hours than were actually paid. Leave days now have the unpaid meal break deducted the same way a worked day does, and start and finish times show the rostered times your pay is calculated from. Your actual clock times are unchanged and still recorded against the day. This corrects what the timesheet says, not what anyone was paid — those figures have always been what MYOB paid. A day rostered as two separate blocks now also matches each block to its own times rather than the first block of the day.
v2.4
24 August 2026 — Consistent Thresholds & Export Warnings
The kiosk now uses the grace period set in Settings. It had been using a fixed 10 minutes regardless of that setting, so anyone clocking in or out 6–10 minutes away from their rostered time was never asked for a reason — while the timesheet still treated the day as needing a decision and would not allow it to be locked. Those days can now be resolved as they arise, and if the prompts become excessive the grace period can be adjusted per role in Settings, which the kiosk now picks up within a minute. Give-away permissions set in Settings are applied at the kiosk too, where previously every role was treated as allowed. Downloading the fortnightly timesheet PDFs now warns when a day is still unlocked or has no clock-in recorded, matching the warning the MYOB export already gave — the two, along with pushing a period to staff, now share a single definition of what “not yet finalised” means.
v2.3
21 August 2026 — Requests Joined Up With Timesheets
Outstanding shift swaps, give-aways, cover pick-ups and leave requests now show up where their consequences land. A timesheet row for a day with an unactioned request carries a marker on the right — “Give-away → Claire Emmons” on the person handing the shift over, “Covering Samantha Melmoth” on the person picking it up — and clicking it opens that request. The same marker appears on clock-in notes, so a note is never acknowledged while the roster still says something different. Requests that expired without ever reaching a manager are flagged too. Each pending request now states what actually happened on the day: who clocked in, who did not, and who was paid without clocking in. Pushing a pay period to staff warns if unactioned requests still affect it. Approving leave for a day already worked no longer writes a leave record on top of the worked hours, which would have paid that block twice — split shifts, where one block is worked and the other is leave, are unaffected. Acknowledging a note is now recorded in the change log like every other timesheet edit.
v2.2
20 August 2026 — Pay Accuracy & Data Protection
Split shifts are now paid correctly. A day rostered as two separate blocks was exporting only one of them to MYOB, so the second block went unpaid — two days were affected and have been corrected. Leave taken on a split-shift day now attaches properly to each block instead of showing as an unexplained missing clock-in, and swapping or giving away a clinic shift no longer quietly moves it to the floor lane. Duplicate leave records left behind by earlier roster rebuilds have been cleaned up. My Timesheets in the Staff Portal now shows your two most recently completed pay periods, labelled by date — the current period was never available to download, since it is only finalised after it ends. Calendar Setup is now read-only: those dates are fixed once and changing them would move every past pay period. Behind the scenes, every change to the database is now recorded in a tamper-proof log and backed up off-site daily.
v2.1
13 August 2026 — Staff Self-Service Timesheets
Staff can now download their own PDF timesheet for the current and previous pay period directly from the Staff Portal, once a manager has reviewed and pushed that pay period — no more manual download-and-email. Unreviewed days are clearly marked Provisional so what staff see always matches what's actually been checked. Manager and staff logins now also remember who last signed in on that device, skipping straight to the PIN field on return.
v2.0
11 August 2026 — Go-Live: Paper Timesheets Retired
Running Well is now the sole record of staff hours — backup physical timesheets stop from today. This release also bundles several weeks of reliability work: break-shortfall and late-departure gap decisions can no longer be silently bypassed, late overruns can be resolved right from the timesheet row with Notes kept in sync, deleted roster shifts no longer leave orphaned leave entries behind, and MYOB export now lets you choose exactly which staff to include. Kiosk card-reader focus is now reliable during PIN entry, break notes, and staff requests. Fixed several Windows-specific performance issues causing reported mouse lag on kiosk PCs, and timesheet hour totals are now guaranteed to match the MYOB breakdown exactly.

A break reported shorter than expected now needs a manager decision — pay as reported or pay to the standard break — before a shift can auto-approve or lock, shown with a clear Expected/Actual/Shortfall breakdown on both the Notes tab and the timesheet row. Added late-departure gap handling. Pending Notes tab sorted newest-first. Fixed iOS safe-area and status bar overlap issues on the Staff Portal and mobile views.

Android kiosk buttons no longer clipped by the navigation bar.

Auto-approved shifts now snap to roster times in both the timesheet view and MYOB export. Mobile kiosk buttons fixed on small Android screens.
